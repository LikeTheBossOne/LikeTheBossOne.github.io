Scrapyard Scavenger

A multiplayer zombie FPS created by a seven-person student team, with cooperative survival and a competitive FaceOff mode.

Multiplayer FPS · Team project · NC State / 2020

My role
Team lead & gameplay programmer
Built with
Unity · Photon PUN
View source on GitHub ↗

Building a game as a team

For NC State’s Advanced Game Projects course, I worked with six other students to create a 3D multiplayer game with keyboard and controller support. Our project received feedback from teaching staff and sponsors at Red Storm Entertainment.

As team lead, I reviewed code, performed QA on teammates’ changes, and served as the point of contact with our sponsors.

Watch the 2020 Games Showcase trailer ↗

Movement & multiplayer foundations

I implemented the initial character movement and physics that the rest of the game built on. I also researched and integrated our networking solution, choosing Photon PUN for the project.

After creating the first networked character prototypes, I helped the team build with multiplayer in mind, sharing implementation patterns and networking practices.

Systems that support the game loop

  • Extensible notifications: a system that lets separate gameplay features register and display their own notification streams.
  • Unlockable debris: players spend experience to open sections of the map, with linked obstacles opening access to the same area.
  • Collectable resources: configurable spawning volumes distribute resources for crafting weapons and items.

View the notification-system example ↗

A second way to play

I implemented FaceOff, a competitive mode that shifts the focus from fighting zombies to fighting other players. Inspired by tactical shooters, it gave the team a second way to use the movement, networking, and combat foundations we had built.

The mode went through team code review and QA before the final presentation, and was played by other groups testing our game.
