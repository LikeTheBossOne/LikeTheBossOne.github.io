## Setup and interpretation

The scenario used scripted travel, invulnerability and learned abilities. It
activated Guard and Magic Missile and recorded an actual enemy death through the
game's combat/event machinery. A development-only schema forced a positive
achievement decision, and the request asked for useful custom equipment. The
model supplied the prose and supported item properties. Production validation,
reward persistence, explicit box opening and inventory delivery still ran.

The reactive quest was published through the normal quest runtime. The scene and
available actions were staged; the quest title, introduction and objective choice
were generated online. Saved-world recaptures disabled new inference and retained
the generated content. These are original game screenshots, without image edits.
