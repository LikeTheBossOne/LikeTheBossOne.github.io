GenAI Minecraft RPG

Minecraft mod · Java · NeoForge

A dungeon that remembers
how you play.

I’m building a primarily single-player dungeon RPG inside Minecraft: branching floors, unusual character builds, and skills that grow through use.

The goal is a world that responds to your choices. Fight, sneak, disarm a trap, or find a clever way through. Then see those decisions shape what happens next.

The planned AI layer

These features are on the roadmap.

A director with memory

An OpenRouter-powered director grounded in actual campaign events, with a knowing, darkly funny voice and callbacks to your choices.

Stories shaped by play

Planned storylines and branching quests that adapt to your character, with different actions, solutions, and consequences.

New rules to discover

An experiment in AI-proposed gameplay mechanics: combine supported triggers, conditions, and effects into new situations the game can validate and run.

I’m a game developer interested in the systems behind a great player experience. My work spans multiplayer gameplay, custom engines, and tools for the people making games.

I’m especially interested in GenAI as both a development tool and part of the player experience. I want to explore how it can help developers build and iterate, and how AI directors could turn player choices into new stories, quests, and gameplay mechanics. Those ideas are driving my GenAI Minecraft RPG project.

I graduated from NC State University in 2020 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a concentration in Game Development.
