GenAI Dungeon Crawler

Minecraft Neoforge mod

A dungeon with
a mind of its own.

A single-player dungeon RPG inside Minecraft, inspired by Matt Dinniman’s LitRPG series Dungeon Crawler Carl. Survival, spectacle, and your relationship with the dungeon’s AI host shape the experience.

The Living AI Director is an opinionated host with a persistent personality and campaign memory. It uses generative AI to shape each floor’s story, react to your choices, and adapt encounters you have yet to discover.

Runtime generated Achievements and Quests

Achievements with attitude

The System AI turns moments from your run into custom achievements, writing names and commentary in its own distinctive voice. It celebrates what you did with theatrical flair, backhanded praise, and a healthy dose of sarcasm.

Quests shaped by your run

The System AI dynamically creates quests around the unfolding dungeon run, giving objectives a story and a voice. Quest names and descriptions carry the host’s personality, turning the next encounter into a theatrical challenge with a clear objective and reward.

Evolving Unhinged AI

An opinionated host whose personality grows more extreme as you descend through the dungeon.

An AI with a personality

The host is a character you build a history with. Outsmart an encounter with an unusual trap, for example, and it might grudgingly praise you, remember the trick, and bring it up on a later floor.

Going primal

Floor by floor, the AI grows wilder until it goes primal. Its personality becomes more extreme, gameplay gets increasingly unhinged, and difficulty keeps climbing through unpredictable encounters, stranger challenges, and higher stakes.
